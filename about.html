ABOUT THE GROUP

Gaussian Splatting Discussion Group

We are a research discussion group at Blekinge Institute of Technology (BTH), Sweden, exploring Gaussian splatting and related research in 3D scene representation, reconstruction, and rendering.

The group holds biweekly meetings to discuss research papers, exchange ideas, and explore developments in the field.
